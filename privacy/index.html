ActKaro

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version: ActKaro doesn't collect your personal data. There's no account, no sign-in, no tracking and no ads. The game works fully offline.

ActKaro is a dumb charades game for Android, made by Sibil Sarjam Soren, an independent developer in India. This page explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. ActKaro doesn't ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or anything else about you. It doesn't use analytics or crash-reporting services, and it doesn't show ads.

What stays on your phone

To avoid showing you the same word twice in a row, the app remembers which words you've already played. Future versions may also save your game settings, like sound on or off. This stays on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Vibration, so the phone can buzz when the countdown ticks, when you score and when time runs out.
  • Internet access, which Android apps built with our tools include by default. The current version of ActKaro doesn't send or receive anything over the internet.

Children

ActKaro is a party game for groups of friends and families. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds something that handles data, such as ads or optional purchases, we'll update this page before that version reaches your phone, and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy or the app? Open an issue here and we'll get back to you.
